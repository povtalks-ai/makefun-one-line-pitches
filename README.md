coded to let an AI agent build their first app idea on make.fun: tell me

the idea, you watch it take shape, and it costs you nothing to try.

Built for the [make.fun "A Day of One-Line Pitches"

## The pitch

> Tell me your app idea over coffee and I'll have an AI agent build a
> working first version for you on make.fun — you keep the idea, you watch
> it take shape, and it costs you nothing to try.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```

`main.py` (standard library only, no network) writes the sentence to
`entry.txt` in the output directory, after checking it is one sentence
of 1 to 240 characters.
